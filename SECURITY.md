# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Do not
open a public issue.

Tesserax rewards them in TESS: 25,000 TESS to start, from 50 to 10,000 TESS a report depending on
its severity. The scope, the rewards, the known issues and the rules are in
[docs/bug-bounty.md](docs/bug-bounty.md).
